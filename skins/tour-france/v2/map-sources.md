# Map sources / attribution

France regions and Île-de-France departments: [gregoiredavid/france-geojson](https://github.com/gregoiredavid/france-geojson), simplified regions and departments, Licence Ouverte. Source files are included beside this document.

Paris arrondissement boundaries: [Ville de Paris — Arrondissements](https://opendata.paris.fr/explore/dataset/arrondissements/), Open Database Licence (ODbL), https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . Source: `paris-geo.json`. The derived geographic layout in `map-layout.json` and the Paris boundary geometry are made available under ODbL. Rendered SVG maps attribute this source through the accompanying catalogue and this document.

Retrieved 2026-11-19. Boundaries are projected and simplified for this learning game. Central Paris and its surrounding departments have independent scales. The Seine is a schematic curve. Leader lines connect display markers to approximate city/district positions; they are not travel routes. The France map covers metropolitan France, matching the existing game stations.

Postcard illustrations were individually generated using the built-in image_gen tool. They are artistic interpretations of landmarks, not architectural surveys. Their prompts and captions are in `prompts.json`; these original illustrations are separate from the geographic databases.

Legacy artwork is preserved in `../legacy-1.9.46/`, including all 53 SVG cards, both SVG maps, their positions and card captions. No legacy learning or reward IDs were changed.
