# Francophone routes — 1.9.45

30 administrative-area levels; 308 original exercises (Québec 204, Belgium 48, Switzerland 56). Separate progression and stable `dlc-` question IDs; the existing 52-stage France route is unchanged. Completion: 6/8 correct, including one transfer question. Rewards: first region clear 20 coins, each new star 5, one destination completion 150; one destination postcard after completing all its regions.

## Pedagogical source

Sonder, *Taking French Seriously*, “Present indicative”, in `Cosonder/Personal-Notes-Project/[F] Taking French Seriously/Taking French Seriously.tex`, revision `1095f9cf4595969d21d887a932544b45d330eec7`. The author requested adaptation. Private notes are not republished: new lessons and examples adapt the N (number) and S (person/stem alternation) framework, with explicit limits on the X/T/doubled-n spelling mnemonics. N/S are learning heuristics, not universal productive rules and not uniquely Québec conjugation. The present-indicative forms are standard French.

Cross-check: [OQLF: irregular present stems](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/24214/la-grammaire/le-verbe/conjugaison/radical-des-verbes-irreguliers-au-present-de-lindicatif), [OQLF: regular/irregular verbs](https://vitrinelinguistique.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca/24654/la-grammaire/le-verbe/conjugaison/verbes-reguliers-et-irreguliers).

## Geographical scope and map data

- Québec: all 17 administrative regions, official codes shown separately from pedagogical level numbers. [Government region list](https://www.quebec.ca/gouvernement/portrait-quebec/geographie-territoire/regions-administratives). Northern Indigenous languages are acknowledged; the course only practises French.
- Belgium: [five Walloon provinces](https://www.wallonie.be/en/stakeholders-and-institutions/provinces) plus bilingual Brussels-Capital. Eastern Liège includes German-speaking municipalities; no claim that the entire province is French-only. Luxembourg means the Belgian province, not the Grand Duchy.
- Switzerland: Genève, Vaud, Neuchâtel and Jura; French-speaking contexts within bilingual Fribourg, Valais and Bern. [Swiss language distribution](https://www.myswitzerland.com/fr-ch/planification/vie-pratique/informations-generales/facts-about-switzerland/repartition-des-langues/). Huitante is taught in Vaud/Valais contexts; quatre-vingts is not marked wrong, and the variant is not attributed to all Switzerland.

Geometries acquired 2026-11-19, simplified and projected to SVG coordinates (equirectangular with mean-latitude cosine correction, Douglas–Peucker 0.7 screen pixels). Québec has a separate south-west inset; map pins are offset with leader lines to keep small regions tappable. Borders are generalised, not navigation data. Full bilingual cantons are shown hatched; the map does not pretend to draw a linguistic boundary.
